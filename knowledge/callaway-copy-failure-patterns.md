# Callaway Copy Failure Patterns

Use this file only for Callaway requests. It records editorial learning from Taiwan Meta equipment work. It does not create product claims and does not replace current product research.

## Required preflight

Reject or revise a Callaway draft before scoring when any answer is missing:

1. Does the hook name a real golf decision or shot situation, rather than a generic performance wish?
2. Does the copy explain what the verified feature means at that moment on the course?
3. Does every performance, technology, loft, and availability statement have a current source pointer?
4. Does the primary text add a buying reason after the creative instead of repeating its model or technology text?
5. Does the CTA match the landing-page action: purchase, specification review, or comparison?

## Failure: generic or official-launch language

### Reject

- `完美解答`
- `雙神器`
- `關鍵一桿`
- `驚人球速`
- `立即探索`
- empty phrases such as `新武器` when no golf situation or product reason follows

### Corrective action

Replace the slogan with a golfer's choice. For example, name the tension between retaining distance and wanting a club that is easier to choose for a narrow tee shot. Then tie it to the verified product feature without promising an outcome.

## Failure: stronger claim than the source

### Reject unless explicitly verified by the current campaign source

- `專利 Step Sole`
- `高起飛角`
- `控球更精準`
- `過草都一樣俐落順暢`
- `輕鬆抓鳥`
- `Driver 的開球距離`
- any wording that guarantees distance, accuracy, launch, birdies, or score improvement

### Corrective action

State the supported design and the shopper's decision instead. For example: a sole design is intended to reduce turf contact, so the shopper can inspect whether the model fits both tee shots and fairway use. Do not upgrade that into a guaranteed turf interaction or result.

## Failure: feature dump or repeated creative

### Reject

- Listing head size, loft, face technology, and sole design without a golf-use bridge.
- Repeating the model name, technology label, or speed claim already printed on the visual as the primary hook.
- Treating any global product page as proof of current Taiwan availability, configuration, price, or campaign terms.

### Corrective action

Use one product reason at a time and translate it through:

```text
feature → shot situation → practical help → reason to inspect
```

For MINI-driver copy, the safe comparison route is the golfer deciding whether the club fills the space between a conventional Driver and a fairway wood. The exact claims, loft, and specifications remain current-source checks.

## Final Callaway gate

A Callaway Meta draft passes this preflight only when it:

- sounds like a golfer making a club-choice decision, not a product launch announcement;
- names a source-backed reason to inspect the current model;
- avoids performance guarantees and unsupported comparative superiority;
- complements the visual instead of reciting it; and
- uses Taiwan-natural golf language with a clean mobile reading rhythm.
