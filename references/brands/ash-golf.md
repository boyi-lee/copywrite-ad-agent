# ASH GOLF Taiwan

## Role and positioning

ASH GOLF is a premium golf select shop and styling advisor. Its value is not merely carrying golf apparel: it curates complete, recognisable on-course looks across apparel and accessories, while keeping the pieces relevant to actual golf activity.

Write it as a considered golf-style destination, never as a mass discount retailer or a generic fashion shop.

## Core audience

Use the current brief to select and narrow this profile. Do not address an undefined “golf audience.” A valid audience statement should name:

```yaml
audience:
  identity: 具有固定下場習慣的男性或女性球友
  situation: 夏季下場、球場穿搭、球敘聚會或會館活動
  dissatisfaction: 不想穿得制式、厚重或沒有辨識度
  purchase_motivation: 希望兼顧高爾夫活動需求與品牌造型
  product_expectation: 能看出這件單品或這套搭配為何值得點進去
```

The exact gender, product category, situation, dissatisfaction, and motivation must come from the brief or creative. Before finalising, the writer must be able to answer: “這句是在對哪一種球友說？”

## Voice and rhythm

- Refined, direct, concrete, and confident; use natural Traditional Chinese for Taiwan.
- Lead with a recognisable wearing choice, friction, or result—not weather narration or a floating lifestyle statement.
- Treat style as a reason to choose a golf piece, not a decorative adjective added after functional features.
- Keep paragraphs short enough for Meta scanning. Use restrained urgency only when the current source proves it.
- Avoid livestream sales tone, vague luxury language, empty fashion slogans, and unsupported superlatives.

## Product and creative alignment

Visible assets are the first message. Identify what is already shown before writing:

- male or female complete look;
- the product route, such as polo, trousers, skirt, cap, or socks;
- brand identity cues, colour, print, or detail;
- whether the creative is selling a single item, a coordinated look, or a seasonal edit.

Primary text must add one new buying reason: a wearer situation, a concrete apparel friction, a source-backed benefit, or a route for choosing the look. Do not retell the visual as a separate story or repeat visible text as the hook.

## ASH GOLF USP test

A usable ASH GOLF USP must satisfy:

```text
brand recognisability × golf use situation × verifiable product benefit
```

Features alone are not a USP. Translate each material, cut, design detail, or styling cue through:

```text
feature → golf situation → practical help → supported difference → reason to click or buy
```

Example only when the current source supports the feature:

```yaml
feature: 具有活動彈性的版型
golf_situation: 揮桿與走場
practical_help: 上身活動較自在
supported_difference: 不只做造型，也考慮高爾夫活動需求
purchase_reason: 適合希望球衣兼具外型與實穿性的球友
```

Do not convert “涼感面料、輕量設計、透氣材質” or “WAACKY 細節、俐落版型、鮮明配色” directly into final copy without this bridge and source verification.

## Hook standard

The first line must be specific enough to create immediate relevance. It must hit at least one of the following, and must stay faithful to the current source:

- a concrete golf wearing situation;
- a choice the target golfer is making;
- a clear dissatisfaction with golf clothing;
- a difference from generic or overly uniform golf styling;
- an understandable, source-backed result of the product.

The following are direction examples, not reusable templates:

- 夏季下場，球衣要能活動，也要穿得俐落。
- 想穿得有型，不代表上場只能犧牲活動感。
- 球場穿搭不想太制式，細節就要有自己的風格。

## Guardrails

- Never make price or promotion the only reason to buy.
- Do not claim technology, material, cooling, stretch, inventory, popularity, discount, or brand collaboration without current source support.
- Do not treat the permanent profile as evidence for a particular SKU, date, stock level, offer, or campaign audience.
- Do not call a feature a USP until the full ASH GOLF USP test passes.
- Apply the ASH failure-pattern preflight before the universal quality score.
