# Callaway Taiwan

## Role and positioning

Callaway is a golf-equipment brand for players who want their equipment choice to make practical on-course sense. For Taiwan Meta copy, write to golfers comparing the next club for their bag: they care about distance, start line, flight, control, versatility, and whether they can trust the club in a real shot situation.

Do not write Callaway as generic premium lifestyle equipment or as a guarantee of a better score. The copy should make the club's role in the bag easier to understand.

## Voice and rhythm

- Direct, informed, and grounded in a recognisable golf decision; use natural Traditional Chinese for Taiwan.
- Prefer verbs and concrete golf language: `站上 Tee 檯`, `架 Tee 開球`, `球道攻果嶺`, `放進球袋`, `比較下一支桿`.
- Lead with a trade-off or shot situation a golfer recognises, then explain the source-backed product logic.
- Keep paragraphs short for Meta scanning. If the brief requests scan markers, use at most one `⛳` or short bullets only to separate genuinely different use situations.
- Do not use em dashes as structural filler. Do not use empty warfare language, hype, or official-announcement phrasing.

## Callaway equipment USP test

A usable Callaway equipment USP must satisfy:

```text
current model evidence × named shot situation × practical choice benefit
```

Translate facts through:

```text
verified feature → shot situation → practical help → remaining comparison question → reason to inspect or buy
```

Examples of valid *directions*, only when the current source supports the feature:

- compact head → golfer wants a more manageable look at address → more confidence choosing the club for a tight tee shot → inspect the model as a tee alternative.
- sole design intended to reduce turf contact → tee and fairway use are both relevant → inspect whether it fits the golfer's long-game gaps.
- adjustable weight or hosel → golfer is comparing launch or shot-shape needs → inspect the available settings and fitting options.

Do not use a feature name alone as the USP. `TRI-FORCE`, `AI`, `Step Sole`, loft, head volume, and shaft name require a plain-language golf use case and current evidence.

## Claim and campaign boundaries

- A permanent brand profile is never evidence for a particular club, technology, loft, price, availability, or promotion. Verify those from the current Taiwan product page, approved brief, or supplied creative.
- Do not call a technology `專利`, or claim it has a patent, unless the current source explicitly says so.
- Do not turn a design intent into a promised result. Avoid unsupported outcomes such as `更精準`, `高起飛`, `驚人球速`, `過草都順暢`, `輕鬆抓鳥`, or an implied distance equivalence with a Driver.
- Do not promise handicap improvement, a birdie, a straighter shot, a fixed launch outcome, or a score improvement.
- For comparison copy, describe the decision need rather than declare the product the `完美解答`, `雙神器`, `關鍵一桿`, or a guaranteed replacement for another club.

## Meta field roles

If the creative already contains the product name, technology name, or a speed claim, the primary text must add the golfer's comparison question or shot situation instead of repeating the card.

- Primary text: name the choice tension and explain the source-backed reason to explore.
- Headline: add the practical role in the bag or next selection question.
- Description: state the next browsing action, such as viewing the current model's specifications or available options.
- CTA: use `立即選購` only when the landing page supports purchase; otherwise use `瞭解更多` or `查看規格`.
