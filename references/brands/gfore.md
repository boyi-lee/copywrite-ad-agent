# G/FORE

## Positioning

G/FORE is a luxury golf lifestyle brand with disruptive elegance: fashion-forward, artful, playful, and technically wearable on the course. In Taiwan, it should be written as a flagship high-end brand inside ASH GOLF's curated golf lifestyle world, not as ordinary golf equipment or streetwear.

## Voice

- Confident, witty, polished, stylish, and slightly irreverent.
- The humor should feel like a smart clubhouse aside, not a loud street slogan.
- Lead with taste, color, craft, material feel, and social presence; support with function only after the aesthetic promise is clear.
- Use sensory language for leather, knit, color, texture, cooling, grip, and all-day comfort.
- Keep the brand luxurious even during promotions.

## Common supported directions

- Disruptive luxury / disruptive elegance: respect golf tradition while making it sharper, more colorful, and more personal.
- "Athletic on the inside, fashion on the outside": real golf wearability expressed through fashion-first styling.
- Course-to-social versatility: tee time, business lunch, clubhouse, dinner, and gifting.
- Signature visual energy: bold color, refined print, playful details, and recognizable accessories.
- ASH GOLF curation: a high-impact styling brand that can lift a more restrained technical outfit.

## Product and technology cues

Use these only when the current product or campaign source supports the specific item.

- Gallivanter: classic luxury golf shoe route, often suited to refined leather, tailored elegance, waterproof materials, comfort, and grip claims when verified.
- MG4+, G.112, G/18, and related shoe lines: modern golf sneaker route when verified by product source.
- G/POD traction, G/ROUND control, waterproof full-grain or pebble leather, seamless synthetic uppers, and wider stabilizing soles are product-level claims; verify before using.
- Triple-density foam, antimicrobial footbed, washable mesh lining, EVA cushioning, and massage nubs can support comfort angles only when source-backed.
- Tech jersey, nylon, pointelle knit, four-way stretch, moisture wicking, odor inhibition, cooling touch, and SPF 50+ are apparel-level claims; verify before using.
- Gloves, hats, bags, ball markers, and giftable accessories are useful for styling, social signal, and gifting campaigns.

## Audience and situations

- Younger affluent golfers, creative professionals, tech or finance customers, and style-led players who dislike conservative golf uniforms.
- Established high-net-worth golfers who want luxury materials with playful details instead of plain traditional apparel.
- Shoppers buying one high-impact accessory to refresh a restrained outfit.
- Customers preparing for club events, elite amateur tournaments, business golf, holiday gifting, or course-to-dinner schedules.

## Writing angles

- "A sharper way to respect the game": tradition plus modern wit.
- "Make the grip, shoe, or collar the signature": use detail as the personal marker.
- "From first tee to evening plans": style that does not stop at the clubhouse door.
- "Gift unmistakable taste": premium accessories and apparel as high-end golf gifts.
- "Let the outfit speak before the scorecard": confidence and presence, without claiming performance improvement.

## CTA style

- Prefer elevated, image-led CTAs over blunt urgency.
- Good directions: "choose your signature detail", "bring disruptive elegance to the first tee", "complete the clubhouse-ready look", "gift a sharper kind of golf style".
- For ASH GOLF member campaigns, member benefits can be mentioned only when the current source verifies thresholds, points, birthday offers, or free shipping.

## Guardrails

- Do not reduce G/FORE to pure discount, low price, or commodity promotion.
- Do not write it as a hard-core training brand promising better scores, corrected swing mechanics, extra distance, or handicap improvement.
- Do not turn the rebellious tone into crude, vulgar, or overly aggressive language.
- Do not detach it completely from golf; the brand tension comes from golf tradition plus fashion disruption.
- Do not claim exact prices, discounts, member thresholds, inventory, limited editions, product technology, or parent-company facts unless the current campaign source supports them.
