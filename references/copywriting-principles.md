# Conversion Copy Principles

## Source hierarchy

Use facts in this order: current campaign brief, supplied landing page or asset, brand profile, general principles. Never let general marketing knowledge override current evidence.

## Persuasion jobs

Every ad must complete three jobs without being forced into one fixed template:

1. Hook: surface a real customer tension, scene, or direct benefit.
2. Persuasion: reduce hesitation with concrete product, category, feature, proof, or selection information.
3. Action: name the next step and use a verified reason to act now when available.

Before drafting multiple versions, state internally the shopper motivation, desired progress, tension, and distinct buying reason for each. A product specification is not itself a shopper motivation. Each version must connect a verified fact to a relevant use moment or decision and explain the buyer payoff; do not fill the body with a list of product features.

Vary the route according to the brief:

- pain -> category solution -> outcome -> action
- scene -> usage gap -> product route -> action
- direct benefit -> supporting evidence -> browsing cue -> action
- choice overload -> curation value -> comparison method -> action

## Content density

- Write what the shopper needs to decide, not what the brand wants to announce first.
- Translate each feature into a use moment and customer outcome.
- Prefer product categories, materials, actions, scenes, timing, quantities, and proof over broad praise.
- Words such as premium, professional, stylish, quality, perfect, and upgraded need immediate concrete support.
- Do not produce a strong hook and CTA with an empty middle.
- Use labels and emoji only when they improve scanning. Do not repeat the same visual pattern across every output.
- Concision is not a quality score. Do not remove the scene, body sensation, object, or buying decision merely to reduce character count.
- Internal strategy nouns such as `需求`, `情境`, `缺口`, `配置`, `狀態`, and `選購邏輯` must be expanded into a shopper-recognisable action, object, timing, or choice before entering consumer-facing copy.
- For Facebook feed placement, write for thumb-scrolling on a phone: keep the hook immediately legible, separate thought units with natural paragraph breaks, and make lists earn their space by giving distinct browsing decisions.
- When the user specifies the opening's line count, write to that count. If not specified, choose an opening appropriate to the audience, asset, and placement. Do not repeat unsupported platform rules about fixed fold position, character threshold, or reading time.
- The reader should not have to decode a framework to know what happened, what it affects, or which category to open next.

## Evidence rules

- A deadline supports limited-time and countdown language.
- Explicit stock or size evidence is required for low stock, final inventory, broken sizes, selling out, or similar scarcity claims.
- A source must support discounts, bestseller status, endorsements, technical performance, and guarantees.
- When evidence is missing, use a neutral next step or season/use occasion instead of invented urgency.

## Field separation

Treat the image or video as the first message. Primary text adds context and buying reasons; headline adds a new browsing or benefit cue; description reduces click uncertainty. Do not echo the same promotion in all fields.
